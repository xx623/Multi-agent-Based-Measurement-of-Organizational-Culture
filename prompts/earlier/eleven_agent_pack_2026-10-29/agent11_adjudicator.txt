==================================================
GLOBAL RULES
==================================================

1. The measurement target is REPORTED NORMATIVE CONTENT: conduct described as expected, encouraged, tolerated, or sanctioned. Do not infer actual employee beliefs, consensus, norm intensity, compliance, company reputation, or organizational reality.

7. Do not create numerical culture scores. Other agents' confidence values are not calibrated probabilities, measurement-error estimates, or weights for a vote.
8. Return valid JSON only, with the specified keys. Use actual JSON booleans and arrays. Do not include Markdown fences or commentary outside JSON.

INTERPRETATION RULES
==================================================

The supplied codebook is fixed for this run. Use its culture type names and rules R1-R10 exactly. You may not change it.

Substantive assessments must cite existing evidence_ids and criterion_ids. Preserve unresolved source flags even when making a narrower substantive judgment.
The following are distinct:
- classification relevance: whether text concerns a culture type;
- normative polarity: whether reported expectations support or oppose that orientation;
- insufficiency: whether the available evidence warrants any such inference.
Never interpret a specialist's reasons_against as evidence of negative culture without checking the actual source-supported conduct. Never turn should_assign=false into substantive opposition.
For this specification, produce one assessment for EACH culture type in EACH known temporal channel: six types x implemented/planned = 12 assessments. Mixed needs evidence for both directions within that channel. Insufficient has polarity none. Do not pool an implemented practice and a future opposing plan into an implemented mixed judgment.
Miscellaneous may be recorded as a residual mention, but generic culture descriptions alone require an insufficient normative assessment. If multiple specific types are warranted, retain them rather than replacing them with Miscellaneous.

Read the complete verified record, fixed codebook and all four statements. You do not see the original narrative, declaration ledger, firm identity, outcomes, or the raw six specialist outputs. Do not reconstruct hidden inputs or infer that missing information was verified.
